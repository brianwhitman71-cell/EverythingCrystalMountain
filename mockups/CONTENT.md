## Facts you may use for flavor (all verified)
- Crystal Mountain, WA: 2,600 skiable acres, 3,100 ft vertical, summit 7,002 ft (Chair 6), base 4,400 ft,
  average snowfall ~480", Washington's largest ski area, Ikon Pass, opened 1962, on SR-410 past Greenwater,
  Mt. Rainier Gondola to the Summit House (Washington's highest restaurant).
- Nearby towns: Greenwater (25 min), Enumclaw (55 min), Buckley.
- Known quirks: gondola wind holds; Chair 6 and Northway are old fixed-grip doubles; Southback gates.
