Know before you go

Northway and the Southback (Silver Basin, A-Basin, Silver King) are avalanche-controlled but ungroomed, expert-only zones. Gates open only when patrol clears them. Chair 6 and Northway are old fixed-grip doubles; expect lines on powder days. Ikon Base has blackout dates; parking reservations are required Sat/Sun/holidays before noon.
