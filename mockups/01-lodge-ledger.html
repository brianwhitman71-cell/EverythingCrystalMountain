Know before you go

Northway and the Southback (Silver Basin, A-Basin, Silver King) are avalanche-controlled but ungroomed, expert-only zones; gates open only when patrol clears them. Chair 6 and Northway are old fixed-grip doubles, so expect lines on powder days. Ikon Base has blackout dates, and parking reservations are required Sat/Sun/holidays before noon.
